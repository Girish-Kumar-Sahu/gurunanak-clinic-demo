About ARC & PARC Clinics

Established on July 1st, 2004, ARC & PARC Clinics is Chhattisgarh's first specialized center for Advanced Rheumatology, Arthritis Care, and Internal Medicine. Founded by Dr. Jagbir Singh and Dr. Shikha Singh, the clinic delivers expert clinical evaluations and patient rehabilitation near CMD College, Bilaspur.

Clinical Services

Rheumatology & Arthritis

Diagnosis and treatment for Rheumatoid Arthritis, Osteoarthritis, Gout, Ankylosing Spondylitis, and autoimmune disorders.

Internal Medicine & Rehab

Comprehensive adult medical evaluations coupled with tailored physiotherapy programs for joint mobility and recovery.
